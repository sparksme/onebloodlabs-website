Our mission

We keep the things a family can't afford to lose.

Jamaica taught us that heritage is a living thing — passed hand to hand, kitchen to kitchen, generation to generation.

Photos fade. Hard drives fail. The voice on the old voicemail gets deleted by accident. Every family loses pieces of itself, quietly, all the time — and most of it is gone for good before anyone notices.

OneBlood Labs exists to stop that. We're the holding company behind a growing family of life-archive applications: software designed to gather a household's memories in one place, protect them with care, and carry them forward — to children, grandchildren, and the ones who come after.

One studio. One bloodline of products. Built so that nothing worth remembering is ever lost again.

Out of many, one blood.

On the nameIn Jamaica, “one blood” is a greeting — a way of saying we are all kin. It's the belief this studio is built on: a family is one blood, and the memories it makes are a bloodline worth carrying forward, whole and unbroken.
