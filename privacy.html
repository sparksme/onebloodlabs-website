Our Philosophy

At OneBlood Labs LLC we are guided by the principle of "One Love"—respecting the sanctity of your family's digital legacy. We strive to provide a secure sanctuary for your memories while being transparent about the technology that powers our innovations.

Advanced Processing & AI Disclosure

To provide high-trust digital sanctuary features, OneBlood Labs LLC utilizes industry-leading artificial intelligence services. This includes integration with third-party APIs provided by **Anthropic** and **OpenAI**.

While we implement strict internal protocols to protect your data during these interactions, users should be aware that processing occurs via these third-party platforms. We operate under commercial agreements designed to maximize data privacy, but we do not maintain direct physical control over the infrastructure of these external AI providers.

Zero-Monetization Tracking

OneBlood Labs LLC maintains a strict zero-monetization policy. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties for advertising. Our use of external technology is focused solely on enhancing the preservation and accessibility of your family legacy.

Media Management

Your family photos and videos are processed to ensure longevity and discoverability. When media is processed through our AI partners, it is done so under strict API-level privacy terms. We do not authorize our partners to use your family's unique media for general model training beyond the scope of providing our specific services to you.

Data Safety Standards
