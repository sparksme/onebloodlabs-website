Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 8, 2026

Our Commitment

At OneBlood Labs LLC we believe that family data is sacred. Our "Digital Sanctuary" philosophy ensures that your information is never treated as a commodity. This Privacy Policy outlines our rigorous standards for data safety and isolation.

Zero-Monetization Tracking Baseline

OneBlood Labs LLC maintains a strict zero-monetization policy. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties. We do not employ behavioral tracking or targeted advertising within our applications.

Strict Family Photo Isolation

All family media, including photos and videos, are stored in isolated, encrypted environments. OneBlood Labs LLC does not use your media for machine learning training, facial recognition, or any purpose other than providing you with access to your own legacy.

Data Safety Rules

  • End-to-end encryption for all sensitive data transfers.
  • No third-party analytics scripts that track individual user behavior across the web.
  • Data retention is governed by the user; you remain the sole owner of your information.

Contact Information

If you have questions regarding this policy or your data, please contact our compliance team at support@onebloodlabs.com.
